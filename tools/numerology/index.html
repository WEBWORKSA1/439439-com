How each number is calculated

  • Life path: reduce the day, month and year of birth separately, add them and reduce again. 11, 22 and 33 are kept as master numbers.
  • Expression (destiny): every letter of the full name has a value from 1 to 9 (A = 1 through I = 9, then J = 1 again); add them and reduce.
  • Soul urge: the vowels of the name only. Personality: the consonants only.
  • Personal year: birth day plus birth month plus the current year, reduced.
  • Chaldean name number: an older system with different letter values and no 9; the total before reducing is called the compound number.
  • Mulank and bhagyank: in Indian numerology, the mulank (root number) comes from the day of birth and the bhagyank (destiny number) from the full date.

Numerology is a belief system, not a science. Use it for reflection and fun, never for medical, legal or financial decisions.
