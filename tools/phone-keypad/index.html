How keypad letters work

Phones use a standard layout: 2 ABC, 3 DEF, 4 GHI, 5 JKL, 6 MNO, 7 PQRS, 8 TUV and 9 WXYZ. The keys 1 and 0 carry no letters. Because each key has three or four letters, a seven-digit number has thousands of possible spellings, and only a few are real words.

Why businesses use vanity numbers

A number that spells a word is easier to remember from a billboard, a radio spot or the side of a van. The usual pattern keeps the area or toll-free code and spells the last seven digits. Shorter words can sit inside a number, too: our finder shows every word of three letters or more it can find.

Tips

  • Avoid 0 and 1 in the part you want to spell, since they have no letters.
  • Q and Z sit on 7 and 9 on modern phones; very old phones left them off.
  • Say the number out loud: some words are easy to read but hard to dial.
