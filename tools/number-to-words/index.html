Number to words and cheque writer

Type a number or an amount with up to two decimals. You get it in words in three English styles, ready-to-copy cheque wording and six other languages.

Three ways English writes big numbers

American style drops the word "and": 439,439 is "four hundred thirty-nine thousand four hundred thirty-nine". British style adds it after hundreds: "four hundred and thirty-nine thousand, four hundred and thirty-nine". The Indian system groups digits differently, as 4,39,439, and reads it "four lakh thirty-nine thousand four hundred thirty-nine", with a lakh equal to 100,000 and a crore to 10,000,000.

Writing an amount on a cheque

Write the amount in words starting at the far left of the line so nothing can be added. In the US and Canada the cents are written as a fraction, "and 50/100 dollars". In India the convention is "Rupees … only", with paise named if present. Draw a line through any empty space after the words.

Questions

How do you write 100,000 in words?

One hundred thousand in US and UK English; one lakh in the Indian system.

What comes after a crore?

Ten crore is 100,000,000. In modern Indian usage, larger amounts are usually said as "lakh crore" (1,000,000,000,000) rather than older names such as arab and kharab.

Is "one hundred and one" wrong in American English?

No, people say it, but formal American writing, including cheques, usually leaves out "and" and keeps "and" for the cents.
