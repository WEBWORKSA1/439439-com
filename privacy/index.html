Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026.

This policy explains what information 439439.com ("we") collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

Information you give us

When you use a form (contact, lead desk, newsletter, support pledge, careers, contest or creator submission) we receive what you type, the page you sent it from, and basic referral information such as the page you first landed on. Forms are delivered to our inbox by the form service FormSubmit. We use these details only to answer you, deliver the service you asked for and run the newsletter or contest you joined.

For lead desk requests, and only with your consent on the form, we may share your request with one vetted partner who can fulfil it, such as a number dealer, numerologist or tutor. We never sell your personal information.

Tools and your browser

Our tools run in your browser. Numbers, names and dates you type into a tool are not sent to us. We store a few preferences in your browser's local storage, such as your colour theme, recent lookups, game scores and an unsent lead-desk draft. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google's Ads Settings, and learn more at how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message before personalised ads are shown.

Embedded videos and fonts

Videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only after you press play; YouTube's own policies then apply. Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.

How long we keep information

We keep form messages as long as needed to handle your request, job applications for up to 12 months, and newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us, or to stop using it. Contact us or email us.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Contest entry is limited to adults.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page with a new date.
