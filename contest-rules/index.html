Official rules: the monthly 439 Challenge

Last updated 8 October 2026.

1. Organizer

The contest is run by 439439.com ("we"). It is a contest of skill: winners are chosen by judging, not by chance.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, donation or sponsorship is required to enter or to win, and none improves your chances.

3. Who can enter

Individuals who are 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live, may enter, except where contests of this kind are restricted by law. Our staff, contractors and their households may enter but cannot win prizes. Void where prohibited. If a contest is open to residents of Québec, we will meet the requirements of the Régie des alcools, des courses et des jeux, or exclude Québec residents for that month and say so on the contest page.

4. Contest period

Each contest runs for one calendar month and closes on the last day of the month at 23:59 UTC. Entries received after that are not judged.

5. How to enter

Use the entry form on the Play and win page. Choose one category: Puzzle champion, Number story or Shorts maker. Up to three entries per person per category per month.

6. Entry requirements

  • Entries must be your own original work and must not infringe anyone's rights.
  • Number stories: up to 300 words, in English or with an English translation.
  • Videos: up to 60 seconds, publicly viewable, with no copyrighted music you do not have rights to.
  • No hateful, sexual, violent or misleading content, and no content that targets a private person.

7. Judging

A panel chosen by us judges entries on accuracy and correctness (40%), originality (30%) and clarity (30%). Puzzle entries are ranked first by how close they come to the target, then by elegance. Decisions are final.

8. Prizes

Prizes for each month are announced on the Play and win page before entries open. Sponsors may add prizes, and will be named. Prizes are not transferable, cannot be exchanged except at our discretion, and may be replaced with a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.

9. Winners

We contact potential winners by email within 14 days of the close. A potential winner must reply within 14 days and, where the law requires it (including in Canada), correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question without help. Otherwise an alternate winner may be chosen.

10. Rights in your entry

You keep the copyright in your entry. You give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to show it on 439439.com, our newsletter and our social and video channels, with credit to you.

11. Publicity and privacy

Winners agree that we may publish their first name, country and entry. We use entrants' details only to run the contest, as described in our privacy policy.

12. General

We may disqualify entries that break these rules or try to game the contest, and may cancel or change a contest if something outside our control affects it. These rules can be updated; the version in force when you enter applies.

Questions? Contact us.
