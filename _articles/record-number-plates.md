A number plate is a small piece of metal, but the right number on it can cost more than a mansion. Here are the records, and the reasons behind them.

## Dubai: P 7 for AED 55 million

In April 2023 the plate P 7 sold for AED 55 million, about US$15 million, at the Most Noble Numbers charity auction in Dubai. The auction, run with Emirates Auction and Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority, raised funds for the 1 Billion Meals Endowment. Guinness World Records lists P 7 as the most expensive number plate ever sold.

## Hong Kong: W for HK$26 million

Hong Kong's Transport Department has auctioned registration marks since the 1970s, with proceeds going to charity. The record for a traditional mark is W, sold for HK$26 million on 7 March 2021. R came close at HK$25.5 million in February 2023. Single letters and lucky numbers, especially 8s, attract the highest bids.

## India: HR88B8888 and a ₹1.17 crore bid

In November 2025 a Haryana plate, HR88B8888, drew a bid of ₹1.17 crore, reported as a national record. Its appeal was obvious: almost every character is an 8, the luckiest digit in Chinese tradition and a favourite across India. The story had a twist, though. The winning bidder failed to pay, and the number was sent back to auction.

## Why people pay

- **Luck.** Repeated 8s and 9s promise prosperity and longevity in Chinese and other traditions.
- **Status.** Single digits and single letters are rare, and rarity signals wealth.
- **Identity.** A birth year, a business name or a lucky number makes a car personal.
- **Charity.** Many auctions fund good causes, which turns a luxury into a donation.

## How plate auctions work

1. **Find the official channel.** In India, the national fancy number portal; in Hong Kong, the Transport Department; in the UAE, the transport authority and its auction partners.
2. **Register and pay a deposit.** Most auctions require a refundable deposit to bid.
3. **Bid within the window.** Online auctions run on fixed dates with reserve prices.
4. **Pay on time.** As HR88B8888 showed, missing the payment deadline can cost you the number.
